Card with Product Card

The default card trigger with data-poltio-floating-product-card-enabled: a second entry point is placed as the second tile of the product grid below. Configured on the element, so no panel rule is needed.

Expected behavior

  • One product card (#poltio-product-card) appears as the second tile and takes the height of its row.
  • Clicking it opens this page's widget panel and locks page scroll; the close button collapses it and unlocks scroll.
  • The floating card in the corner and the product card open the same panel. Opening from one and closing from the other works.
  • No element ids end in -2: a single widget keeps its original names.
