SPA Navigation with Product Card

Client-side route changes on a page whose widget places a product card in the grid below. On each route change the SDK tears the widget down and loads it again.

Panel rule for this URL: any trigger with floating-product-card-enabled on and floating-product-child-number 2.

Expected behavior

  • Before and after every route change there is exactly one container and one product card (see the status box).
  • After a route change, clicking the product card opens the panel and locks page scroll; closing it unlocks scroll.
  • No element ids end in -2.
