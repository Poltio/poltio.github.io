Multi-Widget — Two Expanded Starts

Two widgets on this page are configured to start expanded. Two open panels would both lock page scroll and cover each other, so only the first keeps its expanded start.

Panel rules: a card at bottom-left and a pill at top-right on this exact URL, both with floating-initial-position expanded; the box at bottom-right on https://poltio.github.io/test-cases/multi/*.

Expected behavior

  • Exactly one panel is open on load (expanded: 1) and page scroll is locked.
  • The other expanded widget and the box wait collapsed, hidden while the panel is open.
  • Closing the panel unlocks scroll and brings both triggers back.
