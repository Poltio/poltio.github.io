Multi-Widget — One Grid Tile

A pill can place its own product card in the grid. When it aims at the same tile as a product card widget, only one card may land there.

Panel rules: a pill at bottom-left with floating-product-card-enabled on tile 2, and a product card widget on tile 2, both on this exact URL (the pill is newer, so it ranks first); the box at bottom-right on https://poltio.github.io/test-cases/multi/*.

Expected behavior

  • The pill, its product card on tile 2 and the box render: two widget divs.
  • The standalone product card widget is dropped on position, so tile 2 holds exactly one card.
