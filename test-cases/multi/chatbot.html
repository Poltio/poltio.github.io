Multi-Widget — Chatbot and Pill

A chatbot docks along the whole bottom edge on phones, so it claims every bottom slot. Widgets anchored at the top can still share the page with it.

Panel rules: a chatbot and a pill at top-right on this exact URL; the box at bottom-right on https://poltio.github.io/test-cases/multi/*; the Smeg chatbot on https://poltio.github.io/test-cases/*.

Expected behavior

  • The chatbot and the top-right pill render; nothing else does.
  • The box (bottom-right) and the Smeg chatbot are dropped on position, because this page's chatbot is more specific and holds the bottom edge.
  • Opening the chatbot fullscreen hides the pill until it closes.
