Multi-Widget — Three Widgets, One Quiz

Two pills and a box all show the same quiz. Sharing a content is not a conflict, even for two widgets of the same trigger, and each widget remembers its own close.

Panel rules: a pill at top-left (473) and a pill at bottom-left (472) on this exact URL, both with the close button and a 1 hour remember duration; the box at bottom-right (449) on https://poltio.github.io/test-cases/multi/*. All three show the same content.

Expected behavior

  • All three render: widget ids 473, 472, 449 (both pills share this URL, newest first; the box is less specific).
  • Opening any one loads the quiz in its own panel, sized to its own content; the other two iframes are untouched.
  • Closing the top-left pill with its X hides only that pill and stores poltio-widget-473-closed. After a reload it stays hidden while the bottom-left pill and the box still show.
  • To reset, use the button below or remove the poltio-widget-*-closed keys from localStorage.
