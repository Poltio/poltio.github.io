Expected behavior

  • Storage outranks the server's assignment, so a visitor stays on the same arm across pages and reloads. Use Reset to re-roll.

  • No widget plus = off is the holdout working, not a failure: that share renders nothing and reports no impression. Page-load and purchase tracking still fire.
