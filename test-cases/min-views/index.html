Min Views — Pill

A pill that shows only from the visitor’s 4th page view: the “more than 3 product pages” case.

Panel rules: a pill at bottom-left on https://poltio.github.io/test-cases/min-views/* with trigger-min-views 4.

Expected behavior

Press Reset count first, then open the product links below one by one (a reload counts too).

  • Views 1–3: no pill, no container, no cta-view hit. The view count goes up by one per load.
  • View 4 onwards: the pill shows and one cta-view hit is sent per load.
  • The count is per widget, so loads of multi.html count towards it too.
  • A signed ?poltio_preview= link from the Widget Doctor shows the pill on any view and does not count.
