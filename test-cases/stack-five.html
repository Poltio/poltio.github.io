Multi Trigger — Five Cards

One stack trigger (poltio_stack.js) holding the most cards it takes, five, each with its own color, image and content, opening in a modal. Starts active at bottom-left. Configured on the element, so no panel rule is needed.

Expected behavior

  • On load the cards cascade in: Sun Care in front, then the other four behind it, alternating between peeking above and peeking toward the middle of the screen (to the right here). Each back card shows its own title and round image.
  • One click on any of the five cards opens that card's own content. Closing it brings the cards back; opening another card loads that card's content instead.
  • The contents: Sun Care, Sofa Finder, Dishwasher, Vacuum (opens in Turkish) and Speakers (a draft content).
  • The × folds the cards into a pill showing Sun Care. The pill opens the cards again. After a reload the trigger still starts as the pill (remembered 48 hours; clear site data to reset).
  • Network: one cta-view per card content on load, five in all.
  • On a phone the cards scale down and all five stay on screen; with reduced motion turned on nothing animates.
