Multi Trigger — Expanded

One stack trigger (poltio_stack.js) holding three glass cards, each opening its own content in a modal. Starts active at bottom-right. Configured on the element, so no panel rule is needed.

Expected behavior

  • On load the cards cascade in: Skincare in front, Sun Care peeking above it with its title, Hair Care peeking on the left with its title running up the edge. A soft glow crosses the cards three times, then stops.
  • One click on any card, front or back, opens that card's own content. Closing it brings the cards back; opening another card loads that card's content instead.
  • Sun Care has no image: its round area shows the card color. On desktop a card lifts slightly on hover.
  • The × folds the cards into a pill showing Skincare. The pill opens the cards again. After a reload the trigger still starts as the pill (remembered 48 hours; clear site data to reset).
  • Network: one cta-view per card content on load.
  • On a phone the cards scale down and keep the same fan; with reduced motion turned on nothing animates.
