Multi Trigger — Expanded

One stack trigger (poltio_stack.js) holding three glass cards, each opening its own content in a modal. Configured by the dynamic widget "multi trigger - expanded (stack)" on this URL (publisher 5431023): format Expanded, position bottom-right, starting state active, display modal, and three cards on the Lunea Lab mock-up contents — Shopping Assistant (Skincare), Sun Care and Hair Care — each with a product photo uploaded as its card image.

Expected behavior

  • On load the glass cards cascade in: Shopping Assistant in front, Sun Care peeking above it with its label and its photo on the corner, Hair Care peeking on the left with its label running up the edge and its photo on the edge. A light sweeps across the glass three times, then stops.
  • One click on any card, front or back, opens that card's own content. Closing it brings the cards back; opening another card loads that card's content instead.
  • On desktop a card lifts slightly on hover.
  • The × folds the cards into a pill showing Shopping Assistant. The pill opens the cards again. After a reload the trigger still starts as the pill (remembered 48 hours; clear site data to reset).
  • Network: one cta-view per card content on load.
  • On a phone the cards scale down and keep the same layout; with reduced motion turned on nothing animates.
