SPA Navigation during an Expanded Delay

A widget that starts expanded after a delay. Changing route before the delay ends tears it down mid-wait.

Panel rule for this URL: floating-initial-position expanded with floating-expanded-delay 5000.

Expected behavior

  • With no route change the panel slides in about 5 seconds after load and locks page scroll.
  • Click Change route within those 5 seconds: the old delay veil is gone, and the panel opens once, about 5 seconds after the new load, never twice.
  • Closing the panel unlocks page scroll, and the status box shows one container and no delay veil.
