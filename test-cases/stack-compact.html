Multi Trigger — Compact

The same three cards as pills (floating-stack-format: compact) at bottom-left, opening in a slideover. Starts collapsed, so it shows the pill first. Configured by the dynamic widget "multi trigger - compact (stack)" on this URL (publisher 5431023): format Compact, position bottom-left, starting state collapsed, display slideover, and the same three cards as the expanded page.

Expected behavior

  • On load only the Shopping Assistant pill shows. Scroll down about 300px and the three pills cascade in, stacked upward with Shopping Assistant at the bottom. Clicking the pill opens them at once.
  • One click on any pill opens that card's own content in a slideover from the left. Closing it brings the pills back.
  • The × sits beside the front pill, on the side facing the page, and folds the pills back into the single pill; scrolling does not reopen them after that.
  • Long titles end with an ellipsis rather than wrapping.
