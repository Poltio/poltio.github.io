Multi Trigger — Compact

The same three cards as pills (floating-stack-format: compact) at bottom-left, opening in a slideover. Starts collapsed, so it shows the pill first. Configured on the element, so no panel rule is needed.

Expected behavior

  • On load only the Skincare pill shows. Scroll down about 300px and the three pills cascade in, stacked upward with Skincare at the bottom. Clicking the pill opens them at once.
  • One click on any pill opens that card's own content in a slideover from the left. Closing it brings the pills back.
  • The × sits beside the front pill, on the side facing the page, and folds the pills back into the single pill; scrolling does not reopen them after that.
  • Long titles end with an ellipsis rather than wrapping.
