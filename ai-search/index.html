AI Search, powered by guided selling

Most shoppers skip the widget trigger and go straight to the search box. This prototype puts Poltio there: the shopper types in their own words, the AI fills in the answers it can infer, asks only what's missing, and explains each recommendation.

How it works

1 · Understand

Free text is mapped onto the answers of an existing Poltio flow. "Kedim var" fills the pet question and "15 bin altı" fills the budget. The "Anladığım" chips are the only state, and removing one re-asks that question.

2 · Ask only what's missing

The remaining questions use the widget's question cards. The shopper can click an answer or type one; either way it maps to a real answer value.

3 · Recommend + explain

Products come from our recommendation algorithm, not the LLM. The LLM writes only the "✦" explanations, in the shopper's own words ("kedi tüyü için…").

No flow? Hybrid search

Categories without a flow (headphones here) go to hybrid Typesense search over the whole catalog. Exact product queries ("Aurix R20") are answered directly, and "Klasik aramaya dön" falls back to the client's own search.

Prototype note: this runs entirely in the browser. Simple keyword rules stand in for the LLM intent-mapping layer, and a small scoring function stands in for the api1 algorithm. "Voltaj" and every product and price here are fictional. Try things like sessiz olsun, daha ucuz göster, robot mu dikey mi? or 1 ve 2'yi karşılaştır in the chat.
